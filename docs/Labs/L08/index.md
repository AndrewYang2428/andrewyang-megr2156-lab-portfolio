- Why does asking smart questions matter?

Asking smart questions matter because it helps people understand the root of a problem, challenge assumptions, and develop better solutions. Instead of rushing to find an answer, asking the right questions allows people to gather information, consider different perspectives, and identify needs they might have overlooked. Smart questions also encourage creativity, improve communication, and help teams collaborate more effectively. Ultimately, asking smart questions lead to better decision making and more effective designs. 

## Problem Statement:

- State the purpose of your design. What problem are you trying to solve? (make sure do list your research)
- How did you notice the problem
- Who are you designing the product for? Who is your ideal user?
- What is your approach to your design?

The purpose of my design is to hold and keep a spare key in a safe, hidden in a place that only you know where it is. This will help with incidents when you accidentally misplace your keys and need to get access to your house, storage, safe, etc... This was a problem that I encountered when I went back home. I accidentally locked myself out of my house and had to wait until my parents came home to unlock the door. These small accidents can get annoying and aggravating at times, especially when it happens at inconvenient times. For this project, I decided to make a key safe for a spare key so that if this were to happen again, I will have a spare key to I can resort to, instead of having to wait for someone or have to replace a lock. This will be convenient for every household because it happens to the best of us. To have a key safe that is small enough to be hidden in tight, small places will make it convenient for everyone.
