### Overview:

For this assignment, I designed a snap-fit artifact that snaps onto a stepper motor (28BYI-48). The motor has brackets that can be used to screw onto a motor mount, so taking this idea, I designed my motor mount to simply allow the motor to snap onto the motor mount using the built-in bracket that the step motor already has on it. My motor mount is 2 in. x 2 in. with a thickness of 0.25 in.
