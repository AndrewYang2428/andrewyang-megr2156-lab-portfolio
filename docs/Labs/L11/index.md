## Validation:

After making some adjustments to my print, I was finally able to get the annual snap fit to work properly. I wanted the fitment of my parts to be tighter to create a better snap fit but overall my annual snap fit design works well. 

## Lessons Learned:

Some issues that I had with this project was the fitment of my two parts to have a snap fit. Working with resin was pretty complicated because the material reacts differently depending on the orientation of your print and the post-processing after you finish your print. The SLA tolerances are unpredictable because there are external factors that can cause conflict to the part. To solve for this, I made the tolerances very lose so that when the material is cured, it will fit correctly.
